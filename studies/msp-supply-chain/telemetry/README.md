# Telemetry (description only)

Internal endpoint telemetry was used to check one question: whether the behaviours that follow high-impact supplier compromises are recorded on managed endpoints, with process and command-line evidence.

What was checked, in plain words:

- that the needed fields are filled;
- that detections can be grouped into the eleven behaviours in `../codebook.md`;
- in how many of a fixed sample of customer environments each behaviour is recorded, counted once per environment;
- data retention and coverage.

How it is used in the article: as qualitative visibility statements only ("recorded across managed environments"). No telemetry numbers are published, and no event is attributed to a supply-chain compromise.

The queries, results, sample frame and behaviour mapping are company data. They are kept in the company's approved location, **not in this repo**. Ask the study owner for access.
